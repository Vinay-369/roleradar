Why Is Coverage So Low?

Industry Reality:

  1. Employers Withhold Salary Data: Most companies strategically omit salary from job postings to maintain negotiation leverage
  2. ATS Fields Are Optional: Even when ATS providers support structured compensation fields, employers don't fill them
  3. Qualitative Instead of Numeric: Companies use vague phrases like "competitive compensation" instead of actual ranges
  4. Regional Variation: Indian companies are less likely to disclose salaries publicly compared to US/EU companies
  5. Seniority-Based Secrecy: Higher-level positions rarely disclose compensation publicly

What Can Be Done?

✅ Technical Solutions (Already Implemented):

  • ✓ Extract from structured ATS payload fields when present
  • ✓ Extract from job description text using regex patterns
  • ✓ Capture qualitative phrases like "Competitive", "Best in industry"
  • ✓ Mark internships as "Paid" or "Unpaid" when mentioned
  • ✓ Display compensation on frontend when available

🔄 Process Improvements:

  • User-Reported Salaries: Let users contribute salary data for roles they've interviewed for or accepted
  • Crowdsourced Database: Build a Glassdoor-style salary database specific to Indian market
  • Company Outreach: Contact employers directly to request salary disclosure
  • Salary Estimation ML: Train a model to estimate salary ranges based on role, company, location, experience

⚠️ What Won't Work:

  • ❌ Changing extraction logic—it's already comprehensive
  • ❌ Calling different ATS endpoints—they don't have the data
  • ❌ Web scraping job boards—they show the same data ATS APIs return
  • ❌ Waiting for providers to fix it—this is intentional, not a bug
